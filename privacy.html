Privacy Policy

Vokori Translator (说得通) · Effective November 18, 2026

Summary

Vokori Translator does not collect any personal data. Speech, recordings, text and photos are processed on your iPhone or iPad and are not sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app uses

  • Microphone: to hear what you want translated and to make recordings. Audio is processed on the device. It is kept as a file only when you turn on Save recordings or make a long recording; otherwise it is removed.
  • Files you share: audio or video you open in the app from another app or from Files is converted to an audio file in the app's storage and transcribed on the device.
  • Camera: to read text in photos. Images are processed on the device and are not uploaded.
  • Speech recognition: runs on the device using Apple's on-device speech framework or the app's own model.

What stays on your device

Recordings and their transcripts, conversation history, your glossary and your settings are stored only in the app's storage on your device. Deleting a conversation removes it; deleting the app removes all of it. Like other app data, it may be included in your iPhone backups, which are handled by Apple under your backup settings.

Language models

The translation and speech models are delivered by Apple through the App Store when you install or update the app. If you use Apple's speech recognition for a language, iOS may also download Apple's on-device speech model for it. These downloads are handled by Apple; we receive nothing from them.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No network requests from the app itself

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

隐私政策

说得通（Vokori Translator）· 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日

概要

说得通不收集任何个人数据。语音、录音、文字和照片都在你的 iPhone 或 iPad 上处理，不会发送给我们或任何第三方。

App 用到的权限

  • 麦克风：听取要翻译的话，以及录音。音频在设备上处理；只有打开“保存录音”或使用长录音时才保存为文件，否则会删除。
  • 分享进来的文件：从其他 App 或“文件”打开的音频、视频，会转换成 App 存储空间里的音频文件，并在设备上转写。
  • 相机：识别照片里的文字。图片在手机上处理，不上传。
  • 语音识别：使用苹果的设备端语音识别或 App 自带的模型，在手机上完成。

留在设备里的内容

录音及其文字、历史对话、术语表和设置只保存在 App 自己的存储空间里。删除对话即删除记录，删除 App 会全部清除。和其他 App 数据一样，它们可能包含在你的 iPhone 备份中，备份由苹果按你的备份设置处理。

语言模型

翻译和语音模型在安装或更新 App 时由苹果通过 App Store 下发。某种语言使用苹果语音识别时，iOS 还可能下载苹果的设备端语音模型。这些下载由苹果处理，我们不会从中获得任何信息。

我们不做的事

  • 没有账号，不需要登录
  • 没有统计分析、广告或追踪
  • 没有收集数据的第三方 SDK
  • App 本身不发起任何网络请求

崩溃报告

如果你在 iPhone 设置里选择与 App 开发者共享分析数据，苹果可能会向我们发送匿名的崩溃报告。可以在“设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 分析与改进”中关闭。

儿童

本 App 不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

变更

政策如有变更，新版本会发布在本页并更新生效日期。
