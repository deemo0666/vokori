Support

Questions, bugs or wrong translations: bkxdsjgma@gmail.com

Which devices are supported?

iPhone 15 Pro or later, or iPad with M1 or later (also iPad mini with A17 Pro), with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later.

Why is the download so large?

About 4 GB of speech and translation models install with the app, so it can work without a connection.

Does it need internet?

No. Translation, speech recognition, transcription and photo reading all run on your iPhone or iPad.

Which languages?

Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Vietnamese, Thai, Arabic, Indonesian, Malay, Turkish, Hindi, Dutch, Polish, Czech, Filipino and Persian.

Is my data uploaded?

No. See the privacy policy.

支持哪些设备？

iPhone 15 Pro 及以上机型，或 M1 及以上的 iPad（含 A17 Pro 的 iPad mini），iOS 26 / iPadOS 26 或更高版本。

为什么下载这么大？

安装时会一起下载约 4 GB 的语音和翻译模型，没网也能用靠的就是它们。

需要联网吗？

不需要。翻译、语音识别、录音转写、拍照识字都在 iPhone 或 iPad 上完成。

我的数据会上传吗？

不会，详见隐私政策。
